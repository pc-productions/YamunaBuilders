WHERE THIS PROJECT IS?

Yamuna Sky City is strategically located in Kulai, New Mangalore, Karnataka, offering a perfect balance between coastal serenity and urban connectivity. Situated just a short distance from the Arabian Sea, the project enjoys a premium seaside setting while remaining well-connected to key parts of the city. The development lies along the NH-66 corridor, ensuring smooth accessibility to Mangalore city center and surrounding regions. Important landmarks such as Mangalore International Airport (approx. 12 km), Surathkal Railway Station (around 4 km), and NIT Karnataka are all within close proximity, making daily commuting and travel highly convenient.

YAMUNA SKY CITY FEATURES

ELEVATED LIVING, ENGINEERED TO PERFECTION

  • The development boasts a soaring 60-story tower housing 299 meticulously crafted flats, complemented by an elevated podium featuring three levels of parking.
  • A grandiose triple-height entrance lobby and welcoming visitors lounge set the tone for an elevated living experience.
  • The building is facilitated by six high-speed automatic lifts, each accommodating 20 passengers, alongside two 24-passenger service lifts (Mitsubishi or equivalent).
  • Strict adherence to Fire Service Department specifications ensures fire fighting systems throughout the premises.
  • Uninterrupted power supply is guaranteed via a 100% backup system, featuring an acoustically enclosed generator for seamless operations.
  • Enhanced security measures, including electronic surveillance cameras and round-the-clock personnel, ensure the safety of residents.
  • Multi-video door phone connectivity offers direct communication between security personnel and individual apartments, enhancing convenience and security.
  • Sustainable water management solutions, such as rainwater harvesting and borewell facilities supplemented by corporation water, underscore the project’s commitment to environmental stewardship.
  • The installation of a Water Treatment Plant (WTP) and Sewerage Treatment Plant (STP) ensures the availability of clean water for domestic use and the responsible treatment of wastewater.
  • Advanced waste management technologies, including Bio Organic Waste Convertor (OWC) equipment, contribute to the project’s eco-friendly ethos.
  • Residents enjoy a plethora of recreational amenities, including a Ground floor leisure zone featuring a jogging track, skating rink, and cricket pitch, as well as a dedicated children’s play area.
  • Ample visitors parking facilities are conveniently situated on the ground floor, facilitating hassle-free guest access.
  • Common facilities on the ground floor include a society room, as well as dedicated rooms for Building Management System (BMS) and CCTV operations.
  • Tranquil shaded seating areas, thoughtfully integrated amidst lush landscaped gardens and water features on the ground floor, offer residents of all ages a serene retreat.
  • The first and second floors cater to the needs of support staff, with dedicated drivers/servants rooms featuring attached facilities, alongside common dining areas and separate toilet blocks for both genders.
  • The third floor hosts an opulent infinity swimming pool, a separate kids’ pool, and a versatile multipurpose court, adding a touch of luxury to the residents’ leisure pursuits.
  • Host events in style at our 3rd-floor banquet hall, complete with separate restrooms.
  • Dive into luxury at our 61st-floor infinity pool with stunning panoramic views.
  • Go green with solar panels on the terrace, adding eco-friendly charm to our property.
  • Get a unique view from our viewing gallery at the LMR bottom level.

WIND ENGINEERING CONSULTANTS

CPP WIND ENGINEERING CONSULTANTS, AUSTRALIA

CPP Wind Engineering Consultants fosters an inclusive culture built on integrity, respect, and collaboration. Guided by the legacy of its founders, the company focuses on continuous growth, learning, and applying expertise to deliver innovative solutions for a safe, sustainable, and resilient built environment.

In partnership with clients, CPP combines global expertise with a forward-thinking approach to address current challenges while embracing future opportunities. It provides an environment where talent thrives—benefiting its people, clients, and society.

EXPERTISE & CAPABILITIES

CPP’s global team comprises industry-leading experts recognized for innovation and technical excellence. With laboratory facilities in the USA (Windsor, Colorado), Australia (Sydney), and Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur), the company delivers responsive consulting services across Australasia, Asia, the Middle East, and Europe.

CPP operates four recirculating boundary-layer wind tunnels across three facilities, supported by advanced in-house model design, 3D printing, CNC fabrication, and high-performance computing clusters for CFD and simulation—ensuring precise, efficient, and high-quality results.

Close-up photographs of the completed model in the wind tunnel

Kuala Lumpar, Malaysia

ABL wind tunnels simulate how wind behaves around buildings, accounting for changes in speed and turbulence with height, as well as the impact of terrain and surroundings. They also allow testing from multiple wind directions.

Sydney, Australia

Why They’re Large

Tunnel size is critical for accuracy. Models must be large enough for precise pressure measurement while maintaining realistic wind conditions, typically scaled 250–500 times smaller than actual structures.

Adjustable elements recreate real-world wind flow, ensuring reliable results. Despite advances in CFD, wind tunnel testing remains the most accurate method for analysis.

ALUMINIUM FORMWORK

MFE FORMWORK (MIVAN) MALAYSIA

MFE Formwork Technology is a global leader in aluminium formwork systems, founded in 1991 in Malaysia as Mivan Far East. The company pioneered the commercialization of aluminium formwork for monolithic construction, enabling faster, cost-efficient, and high-quality building solutions. Over the years, MFE has expanded its presence to more than 50 countries, delivering over 2,500 projects and becoming a trusted partner in large-scale residential and commercial developments. Key milestones include its 2007 management buyout and rebranding to MFE, the establishment of a large global headquarters and manufacturing hub in Kuala Lumpur in 2015, and its 2024 acquisition by Doka, marking a new phase of global growth and innovation.
