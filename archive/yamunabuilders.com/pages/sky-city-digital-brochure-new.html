Yamuna Sky City sits at the heart of New Mangalore — 300 metres from the Arabian Sea, along the NH-66 corridor. Mangalore International Airport is 12 km away, Surathkal Railway Station just 4 km, and NIT Karnataka is within walking distance. Coastal serenity. City convenience. All in one address.
